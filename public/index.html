1 Paste the item

In game, hover the item and press Ctrl+Alt+C (most accurate) or Ctrl+C, then paste here with Ctrl+V. Text copied from the trade site or Path of Building works too.

2 Choose what you need

Untick mods you don't care about, and lower the tier where a weaker roll is fine. Every relaxed tier makes the craft cheaper.

What this version covers

Chaos Orbs, essences (including corrupted-only ones), fossil combinations, Alteration → Regal → Exalted Orb, metacrafting (side locks, multimod, “Cannot roll Attack/Caster Modifiers”), Harvest reforges (approximate), and bench crafts at the end. Odds use the game's real mod weights: exact for one or two wanted mods, simulated for more.

Not yet included: other Harvest crafts, Eldritch currency, veiled mods, Awakener's/Conqueror's orbs, recombinators, beast imprints, Aisling, and the Allflame league mechanic. Prices aren't included, so methods are ranked by chance per try, not by cost.
